# Working Note Protocol

Working Notes are mixed records: materials, understanding, questions, short decisions, practice, reflection, and next actions may coexist. Route known Project notes to `projects/<project>/notes/`, known Area notes to `areas/<area>/notes/`, and uncertain notes to `inbox/notes/`. Preserve source wording and evidence; add a short summary, Properties, 3-5 tags, and wikilinks. Move only when the primary owner is clear; use links for cross-owner context.
